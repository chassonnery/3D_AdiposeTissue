# Description of the movies

These movies compare the temporal evolution of various quantifiers for systems with (in red) or without (in blue) bias in the cells insemination process, all other parameters being equals.

*Volumes.mp4* displays the distribution of the lobules volume as a histogram.

*Morphologies.mp4* displays the distribution of the lobules in the plane Solidity ($S_{lob}$) - Elongation ($E_{lob}$). The areas corresponding to the three morphological types (solid elongated (SA), solid rounded (SR) and branched (Ra)) are separated by dotted lines.
